# Initial website

Mode: Read. Visitors navigate four placeholder sections in English or Arabic.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A minimal bilingual personal site whose entire page content is the requested construction message. Navigation and preferences are immediately available.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the supplied style kit exactly: paper or dark ink backgrounds, blue navigation states, IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Sans Arabic, restrained borders, and the kit’s spacing and focus treatments.

STORY: Visitors recognize Abdulhadi Fatani, choose a section, and see that content is under construction. They can switch language or theme without losing their section.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A centered, maximum-72rem header with name at the start, navigation in the middle, and language/theme controls at the end. The remaining viewport holds one centered construction heading. On narrow screens navigation occupies a second row. No imagery, extra copy, or footer.

FORM: Precisely specified narrow placeholder site; the owner approved direct implementation from the style guide, so no concept roll or generated mockup is needed. The signature interaction is switching to the equivalent page in the other language; motion is limited to the kit’s short control hover transitions.
